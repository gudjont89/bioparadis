KVIKMYNDIR.IS - STRUCTURE NOTES FOR A SCRAPER
Observed Saturday 24 October 2026, from /bio/syningatimar/ (days 0-7) and one
film page (/mynd/?id=18728).

IMPORTANT LIMITS OF THESE NOTES
- I saw the pages through a markdown-converted view, not the raw HTML. I do not
  know CSS classes, element ids, data attributes or whether the page embeds
  JSON. Everything below is about visible content and URLs. Check the raw HTML
  before writing selectors.
- Not checked: robots.txt, terms of service (/terms-of-service), and the
  site's own API page (/forritaskil/, linked in the footer as "API"). Read the
  API page first: it may give you structured data without scraping.


1. URL PATTERNS
---------------
Showtimes by day   /bio/syningatimar/?dagur=N        N = 0..21 (0 = today)
                   No parameter = today. The day navigation has 22 links.
Language switch    ?kvik_setlang=is | en | pl        (default seen: Icelandic;
                   effect on labels not tested, so pin to "is")
Film page          /mynd/?id=<integer>
Person page        /nafn/?id=<integer>                (cast and crew links)
Cinema page        /bio/bioparadis/  /bio/laugarasbio/  /bio/smarabio/
                   /bio/sambioin/    (one shared page for ALL Sambio sites)
Trailer            /video_play/?yt=<YouTube video id>
Poster             /images/poster/<name>.jpg   (names are inconsistent:
                   <id>_500.jpg, tmdb_poster_<id>.jpg, <slug>-<timestamp>.jpg;
                   a few films have an empty poster path)
Not examined      /bio/vaentanlegt/ (upcoming), /s/ (search),
                   /kvikmyndalistar/ (lists), /leiga/, /streymi/


2. DAY PAGE: HOW TO KNOW WHICH DAY YOU GOT
------------------------------------------
- A line reads "Syningatimar fyrir DD.MM.YYYY" (Icelandic spelling with accents:
  Sýningatímar). Variants seen:
    dagur=0   "Sýningatímar fyrir: 24.10.2026 (í dag)."   (has a colon, a dot)
    dagur=1   "Sýningatímar fyrir 25.10.2026 (á morgun)"
    dagur=2/3 "... (eftir 2 daga)" / "(eftir 3 daga)"
    dagur>=4  "Sýningatímar fyrir 28.10.2026"             (no suffix)
  Parse the date with a regex such as \d{2}\.\d{2}\.\d{4}; do not rely on the
  suffix or on the colon.
- "Uppfært kl. HH:MM" gives a per-page update time. It differed by day, from
  06:57 to 08:45 across the eight pages I loaded. Store it with each scrape.
- The page's canonical link is always /bio/syningatimar/ whatever dagur is, so
  it cannot be used to check the day.
- QUIRK: on my first attempts, several ?dagur=N requests returned today's page
  (date line said 24.10.2026). The same URLs worked when retried later.
  Always compare the parsed date with the date you asked for and retry on a
  mismatch.
- Navigation labels are Icelandic: "Í dag 24.Okt", "Sun 25.Okt", ...,
  "Sun 01.Nóv".
- Days further out are incomplete: only a few films had times for 29-30 Oct. A
  missing film on a far day means "not yet published", not "not showing".


3. FILM BLOCK (one per film per day)
------------------------------------
Elements, in order:
  1. Trailer link  /video_play/?yt=<id>
  2. Poster image linking to /mynd/?id=<id>      <- film primary key
  3. Age rating: icon frisk/<N>.png where N = 6, 9, 12, 14, 16, or "l" (all
     ages), plus text "Bönnuð innan N ára" or "Öllum leyfð". Some films have
     no rating at all.
  4. "Langar að sjá" (a UI button; ignore)
  5. IMDb link (…/title/tt#######/) with rating text such as 7.6. The link or
     rating can be missing.
  6. An optional bare percentage (e.g. 96%). It has no visible label, so I do
     not know its source.
  7. Title link text = "<Title> <YYYY>": the year is glued on to the title,
     e.g. "Clayface 2026". Strip a trailing 4-digit year.
     An optional word "Frumsýning" (premiere / new release) can follow it,
     including for re-releases (e.g. "Audition 2000 Frumsýning").
  8. Genres and runtime:  "Hrollvekja, Spennutryllir · 1 klst 48 mín"
     Genres are comma-separated; runtime is "H klst M mín" or just "M mín".
     Runtime can be missing (film id 18883 shows genres only).
  9. One section per cinema: a heading "### <Cinema name>" then a list of
     sessions.
 10. An optional "Vissir þú?" trivia paragraph (ignore).
Ordering: films are sorted by number of screenings (the page footer says so).
The order of cinema sections inside a film is NOT fixed, so do not rely on it.


4. SESSION LINE FORMAT
----------------------
Markdown form:  - [<time> <tags/hall text>](<booking url>)

Examples seen verbatim:
  17:50 MAX
  13:00 ÍSL TAL Salur 4
  15:15 EN TAL Salur 4
  14:45 Salur 1 ENS TEXTI
  20:00 3D 3D Salur 1                      (tag appears twice)
  19:30 Salur 1 Tvöföld sýning
  14:00 Salur 1 Eldriborgarabíó
  21:00 Salur 1 Partísýning
  21:00 Salur 2 ENS TEXTIjapanskt horror fest   (two tags run together)
  14:00 Salur 1 MidvikudagsbioMidvikudagsbio    (tag duplicated, no space)
  21:00 Salur 1 Tonlist / 23:15 Salur 1 midnaetursyningar

Parsing rules that held up:
  - Time = leading \d{2}:\d{2}. Latest seen was 23:15, same calendar day.
  - Hall names contain spaces, so do not split on spaces. Observed hall tokens:
        Salur <n>   |   A salur   |   B salur   |   AXL   |   Ásberg
        MAX   |   Lúxus   |   Flauel   |   S7
  - Language tags: "ÍSL TAL" (Icelandic dub), "EN TAL" (English audio),
    "ENS TEXTI" (English subtitles). The tag can come before OR after the hall.
  - Anything left over is a free-text event tag (e.g. Tvöföld sýning,
    Eldriborgarabíó, Partísýning, Svartir sunnudagar, "japanskt horror fest").
    Some are ASCII-folded ("Tonlist") and others keep accents ("Eldriborgarabíó"),
    so normalise.
  - NO TAG DOES NOT MEAN ORIGINAL-LANGUAGE. Example: Laugarásbíó's English
    Coyote vs. Acme sessions (15:15, 17:30 B salur) carry no tag, and its
    Polish-language Forgotten Island session appears only as "13:00 AXL".
  - The line has no date, no end time, no price and no seat count. The date is
    the page's date. Sessions in a cinema are roughly, but not strictly, in
    time order.


5. SESSION IDENTIFIERS = THE BOOKING LINKS
------------------------------------------
Every session links to the cinema's own ticketing system. The number at the
end is the best identifier for a session.

  Cinema                        URL pattern                                  Id
  ----------------------------  -------------------------------------------  ------------
  Sambioin Egilshöll/Kringlunni https://www.sambio.is/websales/show/<id>     6 digits,
  (and Akureyri)                                                             ~419026-429958
  Smarabio                      https://eu.internet-ticketing.com/sales/     5 digits,
                                SMAICE/book?perfcode=<id>                    ~27382-29796
  Laugarasbio                   https://bokun.laugarasbio.is/#/book/<id>     5 digits,
                                                                             ~19198-19587
  Bio Paradis                   https://tickets.bioparadis.is/#/book/<id>    5 digits,
                                                                             ~17814-19581

Notes:
  - The Laugarasbio and Paradis ranges overlap, so ids are NOT unique across
    cinemas. Use (cinema, booking_id) as the key.
  - Laugarasbio's own site writes the link as "#book/<id>" (no slash) while
    Kvikmyndir.is writes "#/book/<id>". Extract the number, not the string.
  - "SMAICE" in the Smarabio URL is a constant venue code.
  - IDs matched between the day listing and the film page. For Primetime they
    were Laugarasbio 19453, 19462, 19468, 19474 (24-27 Oct) and Egilshöll
    429654, 429657, 429660, 429663 (24-27 Oct), 429799 (28 Oct), 429845
    (29 Oct). They also matched Laugarasbio's own website.
  - The same booking ids appear on the cinemas' own sites (for example Sambio's
    /websales/show/<id>), so they work as a join key if you also scrape those.


6. CINEMAS AS ENTITIES
----------------------
Names exactly as in the section headings:
  "Bíó Paradís", "Laugarásbíó", "Sambíóin Egilshöll", "Sambíóin Kringlunni",
  "Smárabíó", "Sambíóin Akureyri"
- I saw no numeric cinema id. Use the name as the key.
- The three Sambio locations all link to /bio/sambioin/, so the link cannot
  tell them apart. Use the heading text, or the logo file name:
  logo_sambioegilsholl.png, logo_sambiokringlunni.png, logo_smarabio.png,
  logo_laugarasbio.png, logo_bioparadis.png.
- Sambioin Akureyri is in the cinema filter but had no sessions in any listing
  I read. Sambio Alfabakki is not a Kvikmyndir.is cinema.
- Halls have no ids. Use (cinema, hall_name). Observed:
    Laugarásbíó          A salur, B salur, AXL
    Sambíóin Egilshöll   Salur 1, 2, 3, 4
    Sambíóin Kringlunni  Salur 1, 2, 3, Ásberg
    Smárabíó             MAX, Lúxus, Flauel, S7, Salur 2, Salur 4, Salur 5
    Bíó Paradís          Salur 1, 2, 3
  (A hall with no screening that week would not appear.)


7. FILMS AS ENTITIES (/mynd/?id=N)
----------------------------------
- Ids are integers, roughly in the order films were added to the site, not by
  release date. Current films are around 17800-19000; older films have much
  smaller ids (e.g. Halloween 1978 = 969, The Exorcist 1973 = 1909,
  Hlemmur 2002 = 3989).
- One film id covers all language versions. "Coyote vs. Acme" in Icelandic and
  English is one id (18822), told apart only by the session tags. The cinemas'
  own sites list them as separate events, so do not expect a 1:1 match.
- DUPLICATE FILM IDS EXIST. On the 30 Oct page, IceGuys appears twice:
  id 18883 (no poster, no runtime, no rating; used for Sambio, Laugarasbio and
  Bio Paradis) and id 18750 "Iceguys – kvikmyndin" (poster, 1 h 40; used for
  Smarabio). Same movie, split by feed. Plan a merge table (match on normalised
  title + year, then confirm by hand).
- Titles are a mix of original English and Icelandic ("Forgotten Island" here,
  "Gleymda eyjan" on the cinemas' sites; "Skrímslaskólinn" is Icelandic).
  Match across sources by this site's film id, not by name.
- Some titles contain soft hyphens (U+00AD), e.g. "Ic­eg­u­ys". Strip them.
  Keep everything in UTF-8; Icelandic letters (þ ð æ ö á í ú é ó) are normal.
- Events other than ordinary releases are listed as films: concerts and live
  viewings (BTS, Ozzy), re-releases (Interstellar, The Thing, Halloween),
  double features (tagged Tvöföld sýning), club nights and film festivals.
  There is no separate "type" field; use the tags and the "Frumsýning" word.
- The film page also has: runtime, genres, director, writer, cast (links to
  /nafn/?id=), Icelandic plot, English tagline, scores (IMDb, TMDB,
  Metacritic, Letterboxd), Icelandic release date ("Frumsýnd á Íslandi"),
  US rating, budget/box office, age rating with reason icons, trailer, similar
  films.


8. FILM PAGE "SÝNINGATÍMAR" SECTION
-----------------------------------
- Shows a row of day tabs ("Í dag 24.okt", "Á morgun 25.okt", "Mán 26.okt", ...)
  for only the days that film has sessions (Primetime had six), then the
  sessions for those days with cinema names. Same booking links as the day
  page.
- In my converted view the tab labels were separated from the lists and the
  sessions came out as one flat run in date order. Match them by position, or
  better, read the real HTML where each tab panel should carry its own date.
- Each session link has a title text "Búin kl. ~HH:MM" (estimated end time),
  about 20 minutes longer than start + runtime (19:50 start, 1 h 49 film,
  shown ending ~22:01).


9. SUGGESTED MINIMAL SCHEMA
---------------------------
cinemas   (name PK, kvik_page)
halls     (cinema, hall_name)                          PK (cinema, hall_name)
films     (kvik_film_id PK, title, year, runtime_min, age_rating, imdb_id,
           genres, merged_into_film_id NULL)
sessions  (cinema, booking_id, kvik_film_id, show_date, show_time, hall,
           language_tag, subtitle_tag, format_tag, event_tag, booking_url,
           page_updated_at, scraped_at)               PK (cinema, booking_id)

Runtime parse: "(\d+) klst (\d+) mín" -> h*60+m, or "(\d+) mín" -> m.
Upsert on (cinema, booking_id). A session that disappears from a later scrape
of the same day is probably cancelled or sold out; I did not see how the site
shows either, so mark rather than delete.


10. SCRAPE PLAN THAT FITS WHAT I SAW
------------------------------------
1. Fetch dagur=0..21 once or twice a day. Check the parsed date against the
   requested day; retry on a mismatch.
2. Parse film blocks -> session lines. Fetch /mynd/?id= only for film ids you
   have not seen before.
3. Re-scrape days 3-21 more often as the date approaches (they are incomplete
   early on).
4. Store page_updated_at so you can tell stale pages from fresh ones.
5. Rate limits are unknown. I made about 15 requests over a few minutes with no
   errors, but that is not a test of limits.

12. THE CINEMAS' OWN SITES (SECONDARY)
--------------------------------------
- laugarasbio.is: server-rendered; today's films and times are in the page; film
  pages are /kvikmynd/<Slug>/<YYYY-MM-DD>; booking ids are the same as on
  Kvikmyndir.is. Easy to read directly.
- sambio.is: event pages /event/<id>/<slug> (ids like 3441 for Primetime).
  /showtimes loads each day with JavaScript; my fetch ignored the date
  parameter and returned 14 Oct. Its show ids are the same /websales/show/<id>
  numbers used on Kvikmyndir.is.
- smarabio.is/bio: loads with JavaScript; my fetch returned a template where
  every film showed the same "19:40 Lúxus". Not usable without a real browser.
  Film pages look like /kvikmyndir/<slug>-<6 characters>.
- Because the booking ids are shared, Kvikmyndir.is is the simplest single
  source for sessions, and the cinema sites are best used only to enrich film
  details.
